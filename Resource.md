## PTE Practice Resources

The following websites can be used for individual module practice, sectional tests, full mock tests, and AI-based scoring.

> **Note:** Free access may be limited by daily attempts, AI-scoring credits, or the number of available tests. These limits can change, so check each website before subscribing.

### Free Full Mock Tests

- [Gurully](https://www.gurully.com/pte) — Offers one free full-length mock test with AI scoring. It also provides question-wise and section-wise practice.
- [79Score](https://www.79score.com/pte) — Offers one free full-length mock test, instant AI scoring, and performance feedback.
- [ALFA PTE](https://alfapte.com/pte-full-sectional-mock-tests) — Offers a free AI-scored mock test and access to selected free sectional tests.
- [Language Academy](https://www.languageacademy.com.au/mock-test) — Provides a free complete mock test with an instant score report.

### Free Sectional Practice

- [Gurully](https://www.gurully.com/pte-practice-test) — Provides Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening section-wise practice, although some scored attempts may be limited.
- [79Score](https://www.79score.com/pte) — Includes section-wise tests for focused practice, with limited free access.
- [ALFA PTE](https://alfapte.com/pte-full-sectional-mock-tests) — Provides free selected section tests and additional premium sectional tests.
- [Language Academy](https://www.languageacademy.com.au/mock-test) — Offers separate sectional practice for Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening.

### Free Individual Module Practice

- [APEUni](https://www.apeuni.com/) — Popular for practising individual tasks such as Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Write Essay, Reading Fill in the Blanks, and Write From Dictation. AI-scoring access may be limited.
- [79Score](https://www.79score.com/practice) — Provides question-wise practice across all four sections with limited free AI scoring.
- [Gurully](https://www.gurully.com/pte-practice-test) — Provides question-wise practice for the individual PTE task types.
- [ALFA PTE](https://alfapte.com/) — Provides individual task practice, AI scoring, and custom mock-test options; some features require a subscription.
- [E2Language](https://www.e2language.com/pages/pte) — Provides a free starter account with sample lessons, strategies, and selected practice questions.

### Paid Practice Resources

- [Pearson PTE Official Scored Practice Tests](https://www.pearsonpte.com/pte-academic/preparation/scored-practice-test/) — The most reliable choice for checking exam readiness because it uses authentic questions and Pearson's official scoring system.
- [Gurully](https://www.gurully.com/pte) — Paid plans provide additional full mock tests, sectional tests, question banks, AI scoring, and performance analysis.
- [ALFA PTE](https://alfapte.com/pricing-plans/subscription) — Paid plans provide full, sectional, timed, and custom mock tests with AI scoring and analytics.
- [79Score](https://www.79score.com/pricing) — Paid plans provide more full mock tests, sectional tests, question-wise practice, and detailed feedback.
- [Language Academy](https://www.languageacademy.com.au/) — Paid packages include mock tests, sectional tests, practice questions, video courses, and coaching options.
- [E2Language](https://www.e2language.com/pages/pte) — Paid packages include complete lessons, practice materials, mock tests, live classes, and expert feedback.

### Suggested Use

- **For daily individual module practice:** Use APEUni, 79Score, or Gurully.
- **For free sectional practice:** Try Gurully, 79Score, ALFA PTE, or Language Academy.
- **For a free full mock test:** Start with Gurully, 79Score, ALFA PTE, or Language Academy.
- **For the most reliable final score check:** Purchase a Pearson official Scored Practice Test shortly before the real exam.

> **Important:** Scores from third-party AI platforms are estimates and may differ from your real PTE score. Use them to identify weaknesses and track progress, but use Pearson's official Scored Practice Test for the closest score prediction.
