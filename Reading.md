## Reading

The Reading section has **5 modules**:

1. **Fill in the Blanks(Dropdown)**
2. **Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers**
3. **Re-order Paragraphs**
4. **Fill in the Blanks(Drag & Drop)**
5. **Multiple Choice, Single Answer**

---

### 1. Fill in the Blanks(Dropdown)

- You will be given a passage.
- You should answer the blanks from drop down.

**Important tips:**

- It's a plus point if your vocabulary is good. If not then will be alright too.
- Try to finish this module within 2 mins.
- It is an important module of this reading section.

---

### 2. Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers

- You will have a passage.
- You get a Question of this passage.
- Answer will be more than one.

**Important tips:**

- It's not that much important. So, do not waste much time in this module. 
- If there is very long passage then you can skip this module.
- If there is medium or short range passage then you can give a try. But give 30-40 secs not more than that.

---

### 3. Re-order Paragraphs

- You will get some paragraphs but on wrong order.
- You have to re-order them perfectly.

**Important tips:**

- Try to finish this module correctly.
- It is an important module of this reading section.

---

### 4. Fill in the Blanks(Drag & Drop)

- You will be given a passage.
- After the passage there will be given some words.
- You should answer the blanks with drag and drop of those words.

**Important tips:**

- Try to finish this module correctly.
- It is an important module of this reading section.

---

### 5. Multiple Choice, Single Answer

- You will have a passage.
- You get a Question of this passage.
- Answer will be just one.

**Important tips:**

- It's not that much important. So, do not waste much time in this module. 
- If there is very long passage then you can skip this module.
- If there is medium or short range passage then you can give a try. But give 30-40 secs not more than that.
