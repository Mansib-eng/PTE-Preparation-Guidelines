## Writing

The Writing section has **2 modules**:

1. **Summarize Written Text (SWT)**
2. **Write Essay (WE)**

---

### 1. Summarize Written Text (SWT)

You will have **10 minutes** to complete this task. There can be three types of texts:

- **Normal Passage**
- **Conversation Passage**
- **Poem**

#### A. Normal Passage

This is similar to a seen or unseen comprehension passage.

**Suggested structure:**

> **Sentence 1**(Select an important point from the upper part), and **Sentence 2**(Select an important point from the middle part), but **Sentence 3**(Select an important point from the lower part).

Finally, read the whole summary once to check its grammar, sentence structure, meaning, and flow.

#### B. Conversation Passage

This type of passage presents a conversation between two or more speakers.

**Suggested structure:**

> While Speaker 1 explains [main point from Speaker 1], Speaker 2 acknowledges [main point from Speaker 2], and both agree/disagree that [shared or opposing point].

Finally, read the summary once to check its grammar, sentence structure, meaning, and flow.

#### C. Poem

Read the entire poem first to identify its central topic and message.

**Suggested structure:**

> The poem discusses [topic] by explaining [main idea], describing [point from the upper part] and [point from the middle part], while emphasizing that [point from the lower part], ultimately conveying that [final message from the last two or three lines].

**Important points:**

- Do not copy incomplete or broken poetic lines.
- Combine related ideas to form a complete and meaningful sentence.
- Select points that accurately represent the poem's message.
- Make sure all parts of the summary connect naturally.
- Read the final summary once to check its grammar, sentence structure, meaning, and flow.

---

### 2. Write Essay (WE)

You will have **20 minutes** to write an essay based on a given question.

Organize your answer into four main parts:

#### A. Introduction

- Introduce the topic.
- Briefly present both sides of the argument.
- State the main focus of the essay.

#### B. First Body Paragraph: Agree

- Main point
- Supporting point
- Relevant example
- Brief concluding sentence

#### C. Second Body Paragraph: Disagree

- Main point
- Supporting point
- Relevant example
- Brief concluding sentence

#### D. Conclusion

- Summarize the main arguments.
- Clearly state your final opinion or overall position.
